NUS AI Engineer Hackathon / 8-hour build

AI Study Assistant

A working AI study product built under an eight-hour constraint, turning uploaded learning material into revision tools and interactive tutoring.
8 hoursBuild constraint
4 modesStudy workflow
01 / Problem

Too much material, too little time

Students often have notes, slides and documents but still need to decide what to prioritise and how to turn passive material into active revision.

02 / Product

Documents become study actions

The product lets users upload learning material, identify likely high-priority topics, generate flashcards and quizzes, and interact with an AI tutor.

03 / Build

A complete product loop

I connected frontend, backend and AI features using Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, the OpenAI API and Framer Motion within the hackathon window.

04 / Iteration

Evaluate outputs, not just prompts

The build involved testing generated content and refining prompts and interactions to improve relevance, consistency and usefulness.

05 / Takeaway

Speed with judgement

The constraint forced prioritisation: build the smallest complete workflow that solves the user problem, then improve output quality.
