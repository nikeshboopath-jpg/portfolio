01 / Context

The setting

My internship sits inside enterprise software delivery, where reliability, repeatability and release confidence matter as much as raw speed.

02 / Problem

A workflow consuming days

A repeatable testing workflow required two people over roughly two days, making it a clear candidate for automation and process improvement.

03 / Contribution

From testing to automation

I developed and modified automation code, managed functional, regression, sanity and UAT work, investigated defects and collaborated with developers, business analysts and QA engineers across release cycles.

04 / Result

Days reduced to hours

The automated workflow reduced execution time to under two hours while improving repeatability and reducing manual effort.

05 / Takeaway

Automate the bottleneck

The strongest automation work starts with a real operational bottleneck and ends with an outcome that can be measured.
